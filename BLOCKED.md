# External blockers

> Only record an external credential, unavailable physical device, required
> human approval, payment that would charge money, legal identity document, or
> third-party outage when no alternative path exists. Ordinary incomplete work,
> a failing test, an unselected preference, or an unverified claim is not a
> blocker.

## Entry format

When a genuine blocker appears, append:

- **ID and first observed time**
- **Blocked requirement and evidence**
- **Exact external condition**
- **Exact minimal unblock request**
- **Non-blocked work selected instead**
- **Owner and recheck command/time**
- **Resolution evidence** (appended; do not erase the original blocker)
